Hold jump to rise higher. Tap again in the air for a vent-hop, then keep holding to inflate and float (costs pressure). Snap near an overhead ring to swing; jump to let go. Dash once per airtime.

Bursts cost 50 charge and break shields. Checkpoint buoys refill hull and air. Falling off the world costs one hull point, not the whole run.

Touch a survivor pod to log a witness. All nine are optional and off the main road. Missing one does not lock the exit.
